# Part 3b-2: Intan placed on the NI's clock by its strobes

Under the NI, Intan has no barcode to be fitted by. Part 3b-1 left such a session tier D (amendment 51). Part
3b-2 places each Intan recording on the NI's clock by matching the two devices' strobe edges. It is the second of
three branches under part 3b. 3b-3 is a partial sync box as the cross-check, and an NI restart is 3c.
- **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-training-records-and-backup-clock-design.md`, amendment 49 (the
  design), amendments 52 and 53 (made while building). Where 53 differs from 49, 53 binds.
- **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-20-part-3b-2-intan-by-strobes.md`, seven tasks, built by subagents.
- **Branch:** `feat/part-3b-2`, from `plan/part-3b-2` (`a5e568d`, the plan), from `main` at `b8d7331`.

## What was built

- **Task 1: synthetic sessions with animal-paced trials** (`f09f77f`). A recipe field `trial_waits_s` gives an
  animal-paced wait before each trial. It is empty by default, so every earlier recipe is unchanged. A new
  recipe, `NI_INTAN_RECIPE` (`ni-intan`), uses it. Back-to-back trials are a periodic strobe stream, which
  cannot anchor.
- **Task 2: least squares from pairs** (`5872523`). `fit_pairs_across` and `fit_offset_to_pairs` fit a rate and
  an offset from matched `(session_s, device_s)` pairs. The barcode fits are the same arithmetic, moved, with
  their messages unchanged.
- **Task 3: the strobe matcher** (`2d85dcc`, `8e0dd52`, `efb1a9b`, `8033451`). `timebase/strobes.py::match_strobes`
  anchors Intan's edges to the NI's and extends the map over the whole recording. Three fix rounds followed the
  task's review. Amendment 53 holds the algorithm as built.
  - Anchor: 2 s windows over Intan's first 600 s; each candidate verified over 120 s; best at least 95% and at
    least 10 points over the runner-up.
  - Walk: refit from 3 pairs over the last 120 s; edges matched within 0.5 ms; one-to-one pairs.
  - After more than 10 s without a matched pair, the walk anchors again, or refuses.
  - Residual limit of 100 µs per block of 2,000 pairs; the whole recording must match at least 95%.
  - A property test: across periods, jitters and burst sizes, no wrong match (refuse, or right).
- **Task 4: both strobe lines read, an Intan recording placed** (`9dbc963`, `dfaf7c3`).
  `events/extract.py::extract_nidq_strobe_rises` reads the NI's strobe bit. `timebase/intan.py::place_intan`
  returns each matched pair on both sides. Edges are cached by file; each rate is read from its sidecar on
  every call.
- **Task 5: Intan fitted by strobes** (`a6f209d`). Under the NI, `SystemTimebase.time_source` is `'strobes'`
  for Intan, and the table gains `n_strobes_matched`. An unanchorable recording is rejected as
  `no_strobe_anchor`, and the others are kept. `TimingProvenance` is unchanged, and tier N holds for a session
  with Intan.
- **Task 6: the NWB file** (`553470f`). Under the NI, `processing/timebase/system_clocks` gains a `time_source`
  column (`clock`, `strobes`, `none`), and the NI texts no longer say every residual is 0. A sync-box file is
  byte-identical (`test_a_sync_box_nwb_file_is_unchanged` passes unedited).
- **Task 7: these records.** Amendments 52 and 53, tier N's line in `lab-host-protocol.md`, wl.works' entry
  in `pending-wl-works-amendments.md`, and this handoff.

## Rulings

Each with what it costs if wrong. The ledger,
`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-20-part-3b-2-intan-by-strobes/progress.md`, holds the full wording. The plan's eight
decisions are in amendment 52.
- **P1, pre-flight, Task 5:** the `synced` session keeps `NI_INTAN_RECIPE`'s waits. Dropping them would need a
  new montage and buys nothing, since the barcode path ignores trial timing. Cost if wrong: none.
- **R1, Task 3:** refit rate and offset from 3 pairs, not 20. Verification stopped depending on luck, so a
  periodic stream's rivals verify as well as the truth and the margin refuses it. Cost if wrong: an early refit
  on 3 noisy pairs could mislead the walk. The residual limit and the whole-recording check catch it.
- **R2:** the whole recording must match at least 95% of its eligible edges, else `Unanchored`. Cost if wrong:
  an Intan with a long stretch of lost strobes is refused, not placed.
- **R3:** the runner-up set holds the best candidate's 8 neighbouring alignments each side. Cost if wrong: more
  verification work per window.
- **R4:** the 100 µs limit is per block of 2,000 pairs against the block's own line. A real crystal's rate
  wanders over 12 h. Cost if wrong: a glitch within 100 µs of a block's line survives (it did globally too).
- **R5:** pairs are one-to-one; the nearer edge is kept. Cost if wrong: none found.
- **R6:** a property test pins no wrong match across periods {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2} s, jitter {0, 20, 50, 200} µs and
  burst sizes {1, 4}, plus the 8 wrong cases of the sweep, and a stream on which only the margin refuses. Cost
  if wrong: test time.
- **R7:** the brief's periodic stream (bursts up to 4) was a code and anchors rightly on 12 of 12 seeds. The
  refusal test uses one word per burst. Cost if wrong: none; a right anchor is the goal.
- **R8:** R3 is kept though no test pins it after R1. Cost if wrong: a little untested code; the final review
  may weigh it.
- **R9:** R2 counts spurious edges, so an Intan with more than about 5% glitch edges is refused. Cost if wrong:
  such an Intan is left out and named, not placed.
- **R10:** the backward pass is seeded with the fit over the forward pass's first 20,000 pairs. Cost if wrong:
  none found.
- **R11:** after a blind stretch the walk anchors again under the same rule, or refuses the recording. Cost if
  wrong: an Intan with an ambiguous stretch after a long break is left out and named.
- **R12:** a re-anchor is made at the first edge with at least 10 Intan edges after it; sparse edges before it
  are left unmatched. Cost if wrong: a real lone code in a long break is not paired; it adds nothing to the fit.
- **R13:** the 10-edge count and the re-anchor's check stop at the next gap. Cost if wrong: none found.
- **R14:** the cause, not the case.
  - (a) The refit uses only the last 120 s.
  - (b) The trigger is device time since the last matched pair.
  - (c) The re-anchor searches within 50 ms of the prediction.
  - (d) The refusal's gap is that same time.
  - (e) Residual blocks are split at re-anchors.

  Cost if wrong: a time-local fit over few sparse pairs has a noisier rate, which only shortens how far it
  predicts.
- **R15:** edges predicted outside the NI's recording are skipped, not re-anchored. Cost if wrong: none found.
- **R16:** a re-anchor takes offsets from every edge of its window. Cost if wrong: more candidates verified.
- **R17:** a 0.5 s periodic stretch after a break anchors rightly; the ambiguous-stretch test uses 20 ms. Cost if
  wrong: none; a right anchor is the goal.
- **R18:** an NI-side strobe loss over 10 s inside its recording refuses the Intan recording. The same loss
  would lose code words, which rule 2 refuses. Cost if wrong: such an Intan is left out and named.
- **R19, Task 4:** the synthetic Intan writes `rhs/<session_id>_rhs/`; the plan's `session_dir / "rhs"` was a
  slip. Cost if wrong: none.
- **R20:** the cache holds only edge indices; each rate is read from its sidecar on every call. Cost if wrong:
  two small reads per call.
- **R21, Task 5:** under strobes, `n_barcodes_decoded` keeps the true count and `n_barcodes_matched` stays 0.
  Cost if wrong: a reader sees barcodes decoded on a strobe-fitted row.
- **R22:** the `two` session is tier D (one Intan file rejected), as any rejected file makes a session D today.
  Cost if wrong: an NI session with one unanchorable Intan recording is not published though its other
  recording is placed.

## Deferred minors

**Task 1**
- The `15.0` literal could be `NI_CLOCK_RECIPE.duration_s`.
- The `ni-intan` recipe test checks only the systems and the wait count.
- `wait < 0` lets NaN through.
- `n_waited` repeats `n_trials`.

**Task 2**
- The equivalence test cannot catch a refactor regression on its own; the existing barcode tests do.
- `OffsetFit.n_barcodes` now also counts pairs (the docstring says so).

**Task 3**
- Margin headroom shrinks for long bursts: an n-word shift matches (n-1)/n, so fixed bursts of 11 words or more
  always refuse. Check the rig's real word counts.
- The helper can overlap bursts at `burst_max` of 11 or more.
- The property test never pins a right anchor (assert that `(2.0, 500e-6, 4, 1)` matches).
- The chunked test compares two chunkings, not the whole (use `scoring_cells=1<<30`).
- The NI-late test comment's "20 s, ten windows" is 17 s, 8 windows.
- The clean test asserts 98%, not every edge.
- The singular "1 strobe edge" is untested.
- Refusal percentages can read 95% while below it (format with floor).
- The margin compare has no epsilon (it errs to refusal).
- Inputs are not validated as increasing and finite.
- Neighbours already among the candidates are verified twice.
- A 126-character line, and two stale helper docstrings (`periodic`, `simulate`'s NI ids).
- The 30x slowdown without R3 is unexplained, and the 12 h refusal worst case is unmeasured.
- R14(a) and (b) each survive their mutation alone, since each covers the other. A burst-heartbeat test would
  pin (a). A 5 ppm rate step keeps 96.7-97.4% of real edges.
- A short leading residual piece (1-2 pairs) is fitted alone, so the 100 µs limit does nothing; merge it into
  the next.
- The out-of-NI-span guard rescans the remaining edges at each trigger; one `searchsorted` would do.
- Steps never reach `MAX_STEP_EDGES` under the 10 s cap (undocumented).
- `REANCHOR_GAP_S`'s comment is stale, and two lines run over 120 characters.

**Task 4**
- `extract_nidq_strobe_rises` duplicates `extract_nidq_words`' sidecar guards, with no direct test.
- The Intan read duplicates `extract_rhs_witness`'s.
- No test of the cache hit or of the INFO log.
- `intan_matched[0]` would `IndexError` on an empty match (`match_strobes` refuses below 10).
- The NI `.meta` rate path has no test, and the `.meta` is parsed twice on a cold NI read.

**Task 5**
- The strobes segment's `start_s`, `end_s` and `first_sample` have no test. Mutating `first_sample` to 0 or
  dropping `+ offset` from `end_s` passes.
- The `two` session's tier, `fit_status` and `n_strobes_matched` (a's pairs only) are unpinned.
- `rate_fit_from_row` gives a strobes fit `n_matched=0`.
- Stale comments: `timebase.py:38`, `:422-423`, `:330`; `core.py:496-500`.
- Each recording is anchored twice per pass (`SystemTimebase` and `Segment`), and an unanchorable Intan is
  searched again on every daemon pass, with its INFO sentence logged twice.

**Task 6**
- No test asserts that a sync-box `system_clocks` lacks the `time_source` column; the unchanged test compares
  texts only.
- `none` has no NWB-level test.
- The new test reads the file three times.
