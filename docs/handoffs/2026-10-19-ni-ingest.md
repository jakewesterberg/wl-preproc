# Taking a session in without the sync box (part 3a)

**Part 3a** of `specs/2026-10-18-training-records-and-backup-clock-design.md`: the sync box should run at
every session, and a session without it is still taken in (the requester's ruling of 2026-10-18). Part 3b,
the NI as the clock, follows; part 4, lab time, waits on wl-xcon's build C7.
- **Branch:** `feat/ni-ingest`, from `plan/ni-clock`.
- **Spec:** amendments 27 to 38 (27 and 28 before the plan: wl-xcon's answer, and the requester's choice
  that a session already processed keeps the clock it was timed by; 35 to 38 from the final review).
- **Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-ni-ingest.md`.

Every line of the plan was proven in a scratch branch before the plan was written.

## 1. What was built

- **A staged session keeps the clock that timed it:** one with no clock row is given `syncbox`, read from
  the database, never the folder (`timebase/clock.py::choose_for`).
- **The manifest:** `syncbox` is no longer required; `expects_rig_record` holds the session until
  `xcon/DONE` is written, a DONE marker listing the rig's files, which are checked on arrival and
  archived against it (amendment 35).
- **The sentinel, discovery and verification** wait for the rig's record when it is expected, and not for
  what a person landed the session without, which the landed topology marks `waived`.
- **`wlpp land-without --session --system --actor --reason`:** recorded in `ingest.LandedWithout` (who,
  why, when), then the session is evaluated once. Any listed system whose transfer has not finished, or
  `xcon`.
- **A session landed without its sync box is never timed by it:** until part 3b its events stage says who
  landed it without and that the NI or lab time keeps its time.
- **The daily report:** a session landed without something leaves the stalled list, and its ingested line
  says by whom, when and why.
- **The archive, reclaim and rehydration** read each system's marker and the rig's, and none of what a
  session was landed without, whose files are stored as they are (amendment 36). The daemon's computed
  stages wait for a session's clock (amendment 37).

## 6. The final review

A fresh Opus reviewer read the branch (`02fd6c0..fb57bc8`) and found **one Critical, four Important and
six Minor**, judging it not ready to merge. The re-grade by effect kept every grade. The requester ruled on
I1 (that `xcon/DONE` lists the rig's files), and the five were fixed, each by tests that failed first and
mutations they catch (23 mutations; 22 caught, the survivor a guard no stage reaches today):
- **`xcon/DONE` stopped the archive, the reclaim proof and rehydration for every session expecting the
  rig's record (C1), and a session with the record alone landed `verified` with nothing checked and could
  never be archived (I1)** (`288f402`, amendment 35): `xcon/DONE` is a DONE marker, the rig's files are
  checked on arrival (`test_the_rigs_record_is_verified_when_the_manifest_expects_it`) and archived
  against it (`test_a_session_with_only_the_rigs_record_lands_verified_and_archives`).
- **A system landed without could still break the archive, with a marker that does not parse or a
  `.bin` without its `.meta` (I2)** (`288f402`, amendment 36): the archive's, reclaim's, the report's and
  rehydration's tests of a corrupt waived marker and of partial SpikeGLX and Intan files, and
  `test_a_system_landed_without_neither_blocks_reclaim_nor_rehydrate` end to end.
- **A session with no clock got a tier-D timing record and parked jobs (I3)** (`e9ab986`, amendment 37):
  `test_a_daemon_pass_leaves_a_session_without_a_clock_untouched_until_it_has_one`, for a session landed
  without its sync box and one never listing it.
- **A relative `--session` was stored as typed (I4)** (`7c8385c`, amendment 38):
  `test_a_relative_session_path_is_recorded_whole`.
- **Deferred, Minor:**
  - a pass that lands the session with its sync box in the moment between the command's check and its
    record leaves a waiver of a sync box that finished; reading the landed topology's `waived` instead of
    `LandedWithout` removes it;
  - the command bypasses scratch backpressure, and records a waiver before refusing a manifest whose
    session id or schema version is wrong;
  - its output: exit 0 for a session still incomplete, stalled or quarantined, without what it waits
    for; this run's actor printed where an earlier record is kept; an empty actor or reason accepted; a
    reason over 512 characters cut silently;
  - a naive `now` passed to `land_without` (the API, not the command) raises inside the stall check;
  - the 1c-2 ingest design spec's topology table does not list `waived`;
  - `LandedWithout` has no foreign key, so a session deleted and landed again keeps its waiver.
- **Set aside by the reviewer, and the rulings on them** (all stand but one): a system whose files fail
  their checksum is a quarantine, not a stalled transfer, so cannot be landed without; a waiver cannot be
  withdrawn before the session lands; a session incomplete but not yet stalled can be landed without, the
  two hours being the report's prompt, not a gate; `wlpp ingest --root` relative predates 3a; 3b's rule
  is 3b's; `LandedWithout` made by DDL on read paths is every table's pattern; `waived` in the topology
  has no enumerating reader. The one that did not stand, the rig's files archived with no reference
  digest, became I1's fix.
